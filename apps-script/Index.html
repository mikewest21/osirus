      The coach

      How it works

      1. At 5 PM New York time each bot gets a report card.
      2. The coach (Gemini, or house rules without a key) picks one setting to change for each bot that isn't already testing one.
      3. A shadow twin copies the bot's account and trades with the change, unranked, every round.
      4. After 10+ days: adopted if the twin is ahead and the sign-flip test gives p < 0.2. Otherwise rejected.
      5. Adopted changes become lessons the bot keeps, even into later seasons.

          Method

          Scoring. Each bot starts with $10,000 of paper money. Its score is its liquidation value: cash plus everything it holds, minus the slippage, fees and capital-gains tax it would pay to sell today.

          Fees and taxes. Every fill pays slippage and exchange or broker fees; stock sales also pay the SEC fee. Tax lots are matched first-in, first-out. Anything held one year or less is short-term and taxed at the higher rate (like a day trader's wins); held more than a year (a year and a day) it's long-term and taxed less. Bots know this: they'll hold a winner up to 30 extra days to reach the long-term rate, unless a stop-loss says get out. Losses carry forward to offset later gains.

          Pattern day trader rule. Like a real US margin account under $25,000, each bot gets 3 day trades (buying and selling the same stock on the same day) in any 5 business days. A bot at its limit holds overnight instead of making a 4th, unless a stop-loss says get out. Crypto is exempt. This is a simplified model, not tax advice.

          Ghosts. Three benchmarks buy once and never trade again: SPY, Bitcoin, and a 50/50 mix. The 50/50 ghost is “the market”; beating it is what counts.

          Skill or luck. Stock portfolios are compared with the SPY ghost on eligible stock sessions; crypto portfolios are compared with the BTC ghost each day. A sign-flip permutation test asks how often pure chance would produce an average that good, then Holm's correction adjusts for testing many portfolios at once. Verdicts need at least ten eligible sessions.

          Conditions. SPY and BTC are classified as Panic, Euphoria, Trending up, Trending down, or Choppy using trend efficiency, volatility, recent returns and Fear & Greed. Challengers learn which conditions suit them and size positions accordingly.

          Grades. A+ means beating the market and passing the luck test. A is more than 5 points ahead of the market, B+ more than 1.5 ahead, B ahead, C within 2 behind, D within 6 behind, and F further back.

          Evolution. Each pool holds its own Reckoning. The top two Stock portfolios breed a Stock Challenger and the top two Crypto portfolios breed a Crypto Challenger; third place in each pool fosters one strength. Genes never cross pools through rankings.
